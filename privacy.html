Privacy policy

Publisher: hears to you · Last updated August 30, 2026

Auraloft does not require an account. We do not collect personal information from the app, and we do not sell or share personal data.

What stays on your device

Auraloft stores preferences only on the device you are using. That includes the last sound, mix, volume, favorites, recents, focus timer choice, and whether Plus is unlocked on that device. The Windows app may also store a local crash log and a “start with Windows” choice. These files are not uploaded to us.

Purchases

Auraloft Plus is sold by the store you downloaded the app from:

  • Windows: the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles payment and your Microsoft account. See Microsoft’s privacy statement.
  • iPhone, iPad, and Mac: the App Store. Apple handles payment and your Apple ID. See Apple’s privacy policy.

We do not receive your card number, and we do not run our own payment servers.

What we do not do

No analytics in the app. No ads. No microphone, camera, contacts, or location access. Sounds and photos ship inside the app, so playback does not need an account or our servers.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. If you open them in a browser, GitHub may process that visit under GitHub’s privacy statement. That is separate from the Auraloft app.
