Privacy policy

Publisher: hears to you · Last updated August 25, 2026

Auraloft does not require an account and does not collect personal information.

What stays on your PC

Auraloft stores preferences on this computer only (last sound, mix, volume, favorites, recents, and whether to start with Windows), plus a local crash log if the app fails. These files are not uploaded.

Purchases

Auraloft Plus is sold through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles payment and your Microsoft account. We do not receive your card number. See Microsoft’s privacy statement.

What we do not do

No analytics. No ads. No microphone, camera, or location access. We do not sell personal data.

hears to you — use the support email listed on the Microsoft Store page.
